## 3. New findings

### QR1 — Low: §6.3/5 and the `pg` row put all of MOD-000's adapters out of `apps/web`'s reach, not only the job store

§3.1 allows `pg` in `src/modules/platform-kernel/adapters/**`, and §6.3/5 says nothing under `apps/web/` imports
`src/modules/platform-kernel/adapters/**`, "directly or through its composition root". MOD-000 owns more than the
job store: register `:149` gives it CTR-TEN, CTR-ERR, CTR-MOD and CTR-FLG, and RFC §7 lists `WP-1A-A0-003`
(tenant context) and `WP-1A-A6-002` (CTR-FLG, CTR-MOD) as request-path consumers. §6.3/1 says only a composition
root imports a module's adapters. So once a request-path adapter of MOD-000 exists (a feature-flag read through
`@supabase/supabase-js`, for example), `apps/web/composition/**` may not wire it, and §8/4's lint rule would fail
the package that writes it. As a guard the rule is decidable; as a layout it over-reaches the stated purpose (keep
the worker credential's code out of the web project).

Condition: narrow the `pg` cell and §6.3/5 to the job store's own path (for example
`src/modules/platform-kernel/adapters/job-store/**`), or say where MOD-000's request-path adapters live. Can be
closed on this branch or carried, by name, to `WP-1A-A0-002`, which writes the lint rule; R0 decides which.

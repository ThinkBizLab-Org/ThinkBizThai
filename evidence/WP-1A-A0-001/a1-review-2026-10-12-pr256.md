## 3. Findings

Stop-the-line: **no**. Nothing installs, binds or applies anything; the RFC is not in effect until the Owner merges.

- **A1-256-1 (Advisory, open, non-blocking). §7's permission has no time scope, where RFC-2026-034 §2.1 and the gate
  text have one.** RFC-034 line 62 opens "Before the G0 exit closes, agents may write application code … against
  contracts at `Candidate` or above"; `CONTRIBUTING_AGENTS.md` line 23 says "Until then". RFC-029 lines 403–404 say code
  "may be merged … once every contract it implements or consumes is at `Candidate` or above", with nothing ending it.
  RFC-034 §3 amends register §5.1 lines 190–191 only "as to writing and merging application code … §2.1", so once the
  exit closes the register's rungs (implementation at `Frozen`, consumer merge at `Integrated`) are what RFC-034 leaves,
  while an approved RFC-029 §7 would still read as a standing permission at `Candidate`, for contracts written after the
  First Slice (Meta connection, billing and others A1 would care about). The effect today is nil: the exit cannot close
  before all thirteen First-Slice contracts are `Frozen`, and §7's own reversibility bullets (no provider bound, nothing
  applied to a hosted project) would still bind such code. So it is not a blocker. **Fix (text, within "ปรับ §7 ตาม
  RFC-034"):** open line 403 with "Before the G0 exit closes (RFC-2026-034 §2.1)," or add "until the pull request that
  closes the G0 exit says otherwise". A0's or the Owner's to take or leave; it does not need new words from the Owner
  because it is alignment to §2.1.

- **A1-256-2 (Low, open, non-blocking). The handoff rewrite drops the only current record that two A1 Lows against
  RFC-2026-029's text are open and owed.** On `main`, `handoffs/WP-1A-A0-001-author-handoff.json` `known_limitations[5]`
  records A1 N1 (owed by `WP-1A-A6-001`), A1 N2 (Q-028-3's answerer and `WP-1A-A0-004`) and A1 N5 (Info). At the head

  history, and the skeleton's A1 reading and `WP-1A-A6-001`'s author would have to know to look there. **Fix (text):**
  one `known_limitations` entry in the handoff (or a sentence in the manifest's approval-increment blocker) carrying A1
  N1, N2 and N5 forward unchanged with their owners. Until it lands, this file is their current record once carried to
  the branch.

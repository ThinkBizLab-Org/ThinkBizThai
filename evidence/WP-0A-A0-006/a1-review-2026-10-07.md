## 4. Findings

### F1 — LOW. Five out-of-package edits, two of them to guard files, have no `amended_by` record on their owners

PR #45 (`1a9f589`) edited five paths owned by WP-0A-A0-002 (2), WP-0A-CON-008 (2) and WP-0A-A0-001 (1)
(§3.3). Its manifest then declared `recorded_on: ["work-packages/WP-0A-A0-002.json"]`, which never held
an entry for this package; `c5a48a8` removed that dangling reference. This increment empties
`amends_without_owning.paths` and its rationale says, accurately, that the five are recorded "in git
history and in that increment's diff" — **unlike WP-0A-A0-004's equivalent text (A1 F2,
`evidence/WP-0A-A0-004/a1-security-reverify-2026-10-05.md` §4), this one does not claim a record that
does not exist.** But the consequence is the same: the owners of `scripts/verify-test-coverage-floor.mjs`
and `test-kits/integrity-manifest.json` — the coverage-floor guard and the digest manifest that make a
guard edit "a visible line in a diff" (RFC-2026-007) — carry no record that another package edited them,
while the same owner records four other packages that did. A reader auditing who changed a guard from
the owners' manifests misses this one.

Why LOW: I read every line of the five diffs and every one is additive or a re-pin of a file changed
additively in the same commit; nothing was weakened, and the edits are visible in git history. Repair is
not this package's alone: an `amended_by` entry is written on the owner's manifest (WP-0A-A0-002,
WP-0A-A0-001, WP-0A-CON-008), so either this branch declares those three manifests in
`amends_without_owning` and adds the entries, or the Integration Owner records a decision that a
merged additive registry edit needs no back-record. Owed by the Author or R0; does not block.

### F2 — LOW. The DATA-DEC-03 blocker's "still open" list omits the two items A1 itself recorded

`open_blockers[0]` lists what stays open "each by RFC-2026-028's status line and migration 173's
header": A1's co-owner acceptance, custody (Q-028-3), the pooler (Q-028-12/Q170-c), 173 pending on the
provisioned instance, no service-cell policy. RFC-2026-028's status line also carries, and the list
leaves out:

- **A1R-2 / A1-173-1**: the production worker runner must refuse a job transaction whose
  `app.workspace_id` is set after `set local role app_worker`, and must check `current_user =
  session_user = 'app_worker_login'` with no `pg_db_role_setting` row for the role — because the role
  can set its own credential and session defaults. Owed to RFC-2026-026's worker half.
- **§5/5's secret-scan half is NOT implemented**: the repository secret scan does not name a migration
  password or verifier literal (Q0 F3), owed to the Integration Owner's path.

Both are security controls on the exact path this blocker describes. The blocker cites its source and
says the remainder "belongs to A1 and WP-0A-DB-00", so nobody is misled into closing anything; but a
reader who takes the list as complete would think the worker identity needs only custody and a pooler
measurement before it is safe to give a credential. Repair: add the two items, or say "including, not
limited to". Record completeness; does not block, and it is not this package's work to close either.

## 2. A1-305-2 (blocking): a line break the reader does not split on hides YAML from the guard

`workflowJobs` splits `ci.yml` on `\n` only and drops every `#`-led line outside the services block. YAML also
breaks lines on a lone `\r` (YAML 1.1 and 1.2), and YAML 1.1 parsers on NEL `\u0085`, LS ` ` and PS ` `.
So one line that the reader sees as a comment can carry further YAML lines. After `          - 5432:5432`:

```
    # note\r        volumes:\r          - /var/run/docker.sock:/var/run/docker.sock\n
```

The whole app-stack suite passes (21/21), and PyYAML and Psych both read the application service as the pinned
container plus `volumes: ["/var/run/docker.sock:/var/run/docker.sock"]`. The same passes with `credentials:`, and
with `\u0085`, ` ` or ` ` in place of `\r` (both parsers agree on all four). GitHub's parser is not run
here; a lone CR is a line break under every YAML version, so that spelling does not depend on it. A CR is not shown
as a line break in most diff views, which is the case the guard exists for.

The same class defeats the new env restriction: on the build step,
`        # x\r        env:\r          DB_TEST_URL: ${{ secrets.PROD_DB_URL }}` passes the suite, and Psych reads that
step's `env` as `{"DB_TEST_URL" => "${{ secrets.PROD_DB_URL }}"}`. It is not new: on `main` (`0fc0e2f1`'s
`ci.yml` and `app-stack.test.mjs`) a job-head comment carrying `\r    services:\r      evil:\r        image:
alpine\r        options: --privileged` passes the suite, so A1-287-2's key allowlist has the same hole today.
Why blocking here: this PR's widening rests on the claim that the service is held byte for byte
(`acceptance_criteria[9]`, the A1-305-1 fix), and it is not; the PR edits exactly this reader. Why not
stop-the-line: `main`'s `ci.yml` holds no `\r`, `\u0085`, ` `, ` ` (counted: 0 each), and planting one
needs a merged governance change.

Fix shape (the author's choice): refuse any `\r`, `\u0085`, ` ` or ` ` anywhere in `ci.yml` (CRLF files
are already refused by the pins, so nothing legitimate is lost), as one whole-file check wired where
`runnerEnvWriteProblems` and `shellProblems` run, with the two insertions above (service and step env) and the
`main` job-head one as negative controls. That closes the new pin and the pre-existing allowlist together.

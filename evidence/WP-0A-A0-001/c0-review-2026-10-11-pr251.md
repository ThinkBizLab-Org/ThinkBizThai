## 3. Findings

Stop-the-line: **no**. Nothing here is in effect until the Owner merges, and every rule in force stays in force.

**Blocking**

- **C0-251-1 (blocking): the guide contradicts itself after the merge.** RFC-2026-030 amended three passages of
  `CONTRIBUTING_AGENTS.md` together. This PR replaces one of them. Two passages that the fast lane changes stay
  as they are:
  - "Verification and handoff", line 59: "an independent Reviewer and Tester must complete their respective checks"
    "Before any commit or push". It also says the merge of an M or L PR "is pressed by a run that is not the
    Author's".
  - "Temporary manual merge control", line 69: "Before the Product Owner merges, the head commit must have … linked
    Author, independent Reviewer, independent Tester, Security/Privacy (when required), and Integration Owner
    evidence". Line 75 says records-only PRs follow RFC-2026-025 §6. Line 79 says "The Author never … authorizes
    their own work".

  After the merge, a fast-lane PR merges on green CI, A0 presses its own, and records-only PRs are fast lane. The
  same file still says the opposite. "Nothing else in the file changes" is met, but that requirement is the problem.
  **Fix:** add these passages to §7, or state in §7 that they stand and how they read under the lanes.
- **C0-251-2 (blocking): amendments the RFC makes but does not declare, and that §9 does not ask about.** The
  "Amends" line (line 12) names RFC-2026-030 §3 and RFC-2026-031. The text also changes these rules:
  - **RFC-2026-025 §5 item 6** treats CI as governance: "A PR that changes governance (an RFC,
    `CONTRIBUTING_AGENTS.md`, CI or a gate) is merged by the Owner personally". RFC-2026-030 §2 says the same and
    adds "either classifier". §2.2 of this RFC instead puts "CI, guards, the integrity manifest, either classifier"
    in the risk lane, pressed by "A0, on the standing delegation". Its governance row leaves out CI. §8 item 2 calls
    the classifier change "risk lane". Q-034-3 asks whether CI is in the risk lane or the fast lane. It does not ask
    whether CI stops being governance.
  - **RFC-2026-025 §6.2.** A records-only PR has one independent reading, which checks transcriptions, Owner words
    and every status move. §2.2 puts "records-only PRs" in the fast lane, with green CI only.
  - **RFC-2026-025 §6.4 first bullet, §5 items 3 and 6** (Integration Owner where gates require it), and
    **RFC-2026-002**'s linked evidence before merge, for the fast lane and for the risk lane. The risk lane has A1
    and Q0 and no R0.
  - **RFC-2026-030 §2**, "the principle, unchanged and absolute":
    - every PR below H has at least one independent reading before merge;
    - an M or L merge is pressed by a run that is not the Author's;
    - the tier is decided "by a script, fail closed … never by the Author's judgement".

    §2.2 has A0 record the lane by hand (line 59). "Everything else" defaults to the fast lane, where RFC-2026-030
    put "anything the classifier cannot place" in H. §3 addresses the presser only.
  - **RFC-2026-030 §6.** A security finding raises a PR to H: "A1 reads it, R0 reads it per PR, and the re-check
    round applies". Line 58 calls this "RFC-2026-030 §6, unchanged", but the risk lane it raises to has no per-PR R0.

  The Owner's option names three things: Candidate-gate removal, CI plus round audits, and A1+Q0 on four classes. It
  says nothing on records PRs, on CI's governance status, on lane classification, or on R0. **Fix:** list each rule in
  "Amends". Where the option text does not cover a change, either make it a §9 question or keep today's rule. Records
  PRs on RFC-2026-025 §6, CI and classifiers as governance, and fail-closed lane assignment can each be kept.
- **C0-251-3 (blocking): the gate change is described inaccurately, and it goes beyond what the Owner was shown.**
  - §1 (line 28) says "Current gate constraint" allows "implementation only on `Frozen v1` contracts". The passage
    actually reads "Until G0 passes, agents may work only on spikes, prototypes, fixtures, fake adapters, contract
    tests, and reversible foundation that does not bind production schema or an external provider".
  - The replacement drops that whole G0 condition. With it go the production-schema and external-provider limit and
    the "Sprint 0A is …" status sentence.
  - §2.1 outranks only "the Decision Register lines that tie implementation to `Frozen v1`" (§5.1 line 190, §7.2
    item 2). It does not name register line 373 ("ถ้าไม่ผ่าน G0 อนุญาตเฉพาะ … reversible foundation ที่ไม่ผูก schema
    เท่านั้น"), which turns on all eight §7.2 items.
  - It also does not name the decisions it supersedes:
    - the Owner's own 2026-10-08 answers (`product-owner-disposition-2026-10-08-g0-exit-and-g1-decisions.md` §10.1,
      the G0 exit closes when the First-Slice set is frozen; §10.3, the gate text and line 373 bind until the freeze
      and the CONTRIBUTING PR);
    - RFC-2026-031 §3.3 and §6.

    It says nothing about what becomes of the G0 conditional exit.
  - The option text and A0's 19:02:46Z message showed the Owner only "ยกเลิก gate 'ต้อง Frozen ก่อนเขียนโค้ด'". The
    Owner did not see the production-schema and external-provider limit.

  **Fix:**
  - Describe the passage as it reads.
  - Name line 373, the 2026-10-08 §10.1 and §10.3 answers, and RFC-2026-031 §3.3 and §6 among what this RFC
    supersedes, and say whether the G0 exit still closes at the freeze.

    text, or put its removal to the Owner in §9.

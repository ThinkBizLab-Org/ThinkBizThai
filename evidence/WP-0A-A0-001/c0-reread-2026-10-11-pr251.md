## 4. New findings

Stop-the-line: **no**. Nothing here is in effect before the Owner merges, and every rule in force stays in force.

**Blocking**

- **C0-251r-1: the fast lane drops parts of M and L that RFC-2026-030 builds in, and the RFC says the classifier is
  unchanged and the fast lane is empty.**
  - **L and M carry reader confirmations.** L holds only if the Reviewer confirms in writing, on the head, that each
    component is behind a feature flag and has no data path. The sources are RFC-2026-030 §2 bullet 4 and §4.3, and
    the classifier prints this on every L result (`classify-review-tier.mjs` line 379). M's "with tests" is the
    Tester's to confirm (§4.1). In a "green CI only" lane nobody makes these confirmations before the merge.
  - **The tier record goes unchecked.** RFC-2026-030 §3.1 has each reader repeat the classifier on the head. CI
    does not run the tier classifier. In the fast lane, only the Author runs it before pressing its own merge.
  - **Amended but not listed in §3:** RFC-2026-030 §2 bullet 1 (an independent reading below H; an M or L merge
    pressed by a run that is not the Author's), §2 bullet 4, §3.1 bullet 3, §3.3 ("L never has fewer than one
    independent reading plus a Tester verdict") and §4.3.
  - **"Today the fast lane is empty" (line 76) is wrong.** `L_PATHS` puts `apps/web` components, locale catalogues,
    styles and public images in L with no module, so `M_ELIGIBLE_MODULES` does not hold them back.
  - **C0-251-1's remnant.** Lines 27 and 74 still say the Author never integrates its own work.
  - The Owner's option ("PR ทั่วไปผ่านด้วย CI อัตโนมัติ") does cover dropping the per-PR reader. The issue is that the
    RFC hides it.
  - **Fix:**
    - In §2.2, say whether L's two confirmations and M's test confirmation move to the audit or stay before the
      merge with a named reader.
    - List the RFC-2026-030 sections above in §3 and in the "Amends" line.
    - Correct line 76.
    - Say who runs the base's classifier before a fast-lane merge, or owe CI running it in §8.
    - Say in §3 or §7 how "integrate their own work" reads when A0 presses fast-lane work.
    - Add the unconfirmed-L/M risk to §4.
- **C0-251r-2: no script decides between the governance lane and the risk lane, though line 64 says the lane follows
  the classifier.**
  - The tier classifier prints H and nothing finer. Records has its own script; governance has none.
  - Line 69 names governance by category: "gate rules", "the integrity guard's own code". Line 71 puts "scripts and
    test kits" in the risk lane. So `scripts/verify-branch-scope.mjs`, `scripts/verify-test-coverage-floor.mjs` and
    the handoff guard can be read either way.
  - That boundary decides whether the Owner merges personally or A0 presses after two readers. Leaving it to the
    Author's judgement goes against RFC-2026-030 §2 bullet 3.
  - Line 69 also cites RFC-2026-025 §5 item 6 for the integrity guard, which that item does not name.
  - **Fix:** list the governance paths: `architecture/decisions/**`, `CONTRIBUTING_AGENTS.md`, `.github/**`, both
    classifiers, and the named guard scripts. Add "in doubt, governance". Have A1 and Q0 confirm the lane in writing
    on a risk-lane head.
- **C0-251r-3: RFC-2026-031 §6 is called unchanged but is restated with a new condition.**
  - §6 item 2 reads: "A PR amending `CONTRIBUTING_AGENTS.md` 'Current gate constraint' is merged". That is A0's own
    stricter reading of the Owner's "มี PR แก้ CONTRIBUTING". This PR is such a PR, and RFC-034 §1 (lines 32–33)
    repeats the condition in the same form.
  - §2.1 (lines 56–57) instead requires "a later PR that amends the gate text to say the exit has closed. This RFC is
    not that PR". §3 (line 119) and §5 (line 134) call §6 unchanged.
  - Read as written, RFC-2026-031 lets this merge meet item 2. The two RFCs would then disagree on what closes G0.
  - The restatement is a narrowing, so no §9 question is needed.
  - **Fix:** declare RFC-2026-031 §6 item 2 amended, in §3 and in "Amends". The amending PR is one merged after the
    thirteen freezes that says the exit has closed, and this PR does not count. Align §1 line 33.
- **C0-251r-4: the recommended answer to Q-034-2 would breach a rule the RFC says it keeps.**
  - Q-034-2 (lines 178–181) recommends naming "each First-Slice module" in `M_ELIGIBLE_MODULES` in one PR.
  - RFC-2026-030 §4.2 item 6 says a module that "owns identity, tenancy, IAM, sessions, secrets, billing,
    entitlement, publishing, connectors, uploads, jobs or provider calls is never added". It also needs a test per
    module class.
  - RFC-034 says nothing about the classifier changes (line 65, §2.4).
  - A literal "yes" would move auth and payment module code to the fast lane, against the option the Owner chose:
    "คง A1+Q0 อ่านเฉพาะ PR ที่แตะ migration/RLS, auth, secret, payment".
  - **Fix:** Q-034-2 asks about timing only (all eligible modules at once, or one by one) and states the exclusion
    and the test condition.

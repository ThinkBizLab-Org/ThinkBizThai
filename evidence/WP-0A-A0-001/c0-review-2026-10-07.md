### F1: the stop conditions of the approved fallback are A0's, and four are weaker than the source (Major)

D0, as the Owner accepted it, says: bind each external item to the gate it actually blocks **(ตาราง §6)**, and
no production customer data until legal/PDPA passes. Plan §6 has three columns: item, gate, why start now. It
has **no stop-condition column**.

Disposition §4.3 and the tracker's new table add a column "Stop condition until it passes" /
"stop condition ระหว่างรอ". §4.1 then makes that column the fallback itself: the gate "is the item's due gate
and its stop condition". Nothing labels the column as A0's. The pass rule D0 invokes is the readiness report's,
and the stop conditions in that same report (§10, lines 194-205) are these:

| Item | Readiness report §10 stop condition | What the record writes instead | Effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| Storage restore/purge | `ห้ามเปิด self-service permanent deletion` | "No storage SLA claim" / `ห้ามอ้าง storage SLA` | The deletion constraint is dropped |
| Skincare domain reviewer | `ห้ามเปิด autonomous skincare publishing` | "No claim rules treated as reviewed" | The publishing constraint is dropped |
| SME usability | `ห้ามอ้างว่า onboarding ผ่านผู้ใช้จริง` | "Tokens/components only as reversible work" (plan §6's "why" column) | The claim constraint is dropped |
| Stripe live | `ห้ามขาย Paid Beta จริง` | "No paid Beta via Stripe" | Narrowed to one channel |

The other rows agree with a source: Meta (§10 "ห้ามเปิด publishing จริง"), legal/accounting (§10), email
(register line 119), PDPA (register line 111 and D0 itself).

Why this blocks: this is the record by which G0 is said to pass. A later reader takes "stop condition until it
passes" in a gate record as the binding constraint. The readiness report still binds as written, but the record
now says something narrower, and it says it in the Owner's name. The Owner was shown no stop-condition text. This
is the pattern the 2026-10-05 review (F1) caught: an Owner disposition must not record more as decided than the
Owner was shown, and that includes loosening a constraint. The Stripe row matters in particular. The tracker
already says that counting Stripe as an approved fallback is A0's reading "ที่ต้องยืนยันใน G0-024". This PR
moves G0-024 to "decided, conditional", but D0 does not mention Stripe, so that reading is still unconfirmed.

**Fix before merge** (disposition §4.3 and the tracker table only):
1. In each row that §10 covers, put the readiness report §10 stop condition **verbatim**, with its line.
2. Label the column, or each A0-authored addition, as "A0's derivation, not shown to the Owner", and cite the
   source where there is one (register 111/119, D14, plan §6).
3. In the Stripe row, keep `ห้ามขาย Paid Beta จริง` and say that the manual-invoice reading is still A0's
   and that D0 did not confirm it.

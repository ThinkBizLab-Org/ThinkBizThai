    for no object: the snapshot's list and digest, and provenance lines.
  - Lines 216 and 47–48 say "most of the 12 would still have been governance", citing Q0-251-31. Q0's measurement
    (`q0-reread7` §2.8) and mine (§2 above) find **none** of the twelve clearly reaching the risk lane under this text:

    - #173 pins tables it did not add;

    Owner the count in chat when asking Q-034-6 and fix the text on the §10 commit.
- **C0-251r8-2. The snapshot's prose and list are placed with inaccurate labels.**
  - `why` is named as a provenance line "a regeneration rewrites". No script writes `catalog-snapshot.json`, and `why`
    is hand-written narrative [measured]. Read strictly, the qualifier excludes its own example. "And the like" can
    reach the snapshot's other hand-written notes (`_measurement_note`, `_role_scoped_policies_note`, …).

  - Safety of admitting `why` prose is A1's call. The gate checks only that `why` holds 40 or more characters (3534).
  - **Fix:** "an entry naming a migration the pull request adds in the snapshot's `not_applied_to_this_instance`
    list (a declared gap the tail rule checks), the `taken_against_migrations` digest, a regenerated `_how_measured`
    line, and a sentence added to the list's `why` for that migration".

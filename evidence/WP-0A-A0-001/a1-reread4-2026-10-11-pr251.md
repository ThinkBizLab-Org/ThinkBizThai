### Advisory

- **A1-251r4-1. "A row for a … moved branch" contradicts the sentence after it, on the one pin that stops a branch
  choosing its judge.** Lines 90–94 send "a row for a new or moved branch in `test-kits/branch-identity.test.mjs`" to its
  file's lane, then say "Removing or changing an existing entry … is governance". `BRANCH_OWNERSHIP` is checked with
  `deepEqual`, so moving a branch always removes or rekeys an existing row. The pin exists because independent review
  twelve repointed `WP-0A-CON-002` at another branch and went green. Bounded: the classifier places `test-kits/**` at H,
  so the lowest lane a branch row reaches is the risk lane, where A1 reads before merge; and governance is by path, so
  moving a package's branch cannot lower the lane of any governance file. Recommend: "a new branch, or the same package's
  branch renamed (its old row removed), follows its manifest's lane; pointing a branch at a different package is
  governance".
- **A1-251r4-2. The database harness is no longer named.** The third revision's "the database test harness CI invokes"
  became "`scripts/db/run.mjs` and every module it imports, the `Makefile` targets CI runs". `run.mjs` spawns
  `scripts/db/rls-smoke.mjs` rather than importing it; `rls-smoke.mjs` imports `authz-proofs.mjs`; CI loads
  `db/foundation/ci/supabase-shim.sql`, which creates the platform roles RLS runs under. None is named now. They are still
  covered by "any script or test CI runs as a guard or runner" and "if it is unclear … it is governance", all are H
  paths (risk lane at worst, read by A1), and CI's negative control fails a gutted `db-rls-smoke`. Recommend: "and every
  script it imports or spawns, transitively, and the SQL CI loads before the migrations".
- **A1-251r4-3. The audit lane and the standing directions each rest on a boundary nobody checks.** (i) Until §8 item 2,
  no script prints the audit lane, so "holds only the round audit's files" (line 88) is A0's own reading of its own
  PR, as blob-equality is; the Owner is told the second, not the first. Recommend, if the Owner chooses (a): A0 records
  in the audit PR `git diff --name-only <merge-base>..<head>` and each file's `git hash-object` beside its auditor's
  source commit, and the auditors' branches are kept until the next audit, which compares them. (ii) Lines 95–97 keep
  §10.2 at "the reach it already has", which is "governance PRs inside the approved G0-exit/G1/G2 plan". Whether §8
  item 2 (CI prints the tier and lane) and item 5 (naming modules), the two governance PRs that open the fast lane, are
  inside that plan is not said; on that boundary A0 or the Owner presses them. All four roles read them either way.
  Recommend the Owner say it when he answers §9, or §8 say they are asked first. R0's and C0's.
- **A1-251r4-4. Naming `foundation-contract.test.mjs` whole as governance pulls data-foundation batches into the full
  path.** The file holds the classifier's pins and the data foundation's own suite (88 tests; batches 173 and 174

  Owner or §10.2 to press. That fails closed, so it is safe and a narrowing, but it takes back much of what the Owner

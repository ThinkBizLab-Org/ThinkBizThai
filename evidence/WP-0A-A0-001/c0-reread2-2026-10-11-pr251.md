**Blocking**

- **C0-251r2-1: the rework deletes a rule from `CONTRIBUTING_AGENTS.md` that §7 does not amend.**
  - On `main`, "Temporary manual merge control" has five bullets. The fifth reads: "Record the PR URL, head SHA, CI
    run, evidence links, and rollback plan in the handoff. Roll back with a reviewed revert commit/PR, never a
    force-push."
  - On the head, the section has four bullets. Commit `2b0bf9f3` replaced bullet 4 with passage (e) and dropped
    bullet 5 in the same hunk. It was present at `5cb6d727`.
  - RFC §7 (e) replaces "fourth bullet" only. §3 (line 135) says RFC-2026-002 items 4 and 5 are "unchanged", and
    item 5 ("Reversible recovery") and RFC-2026-002's "Evidence and review" list are what that bullet carries into the
    guide. The handoff records "nothing else in the file changes" as a pass.
  - After the merge, the guide would no longer tell an Author to record the merge facts and rollback plan, or to roll
    back by reviewed revert. The Owner would be merging a change he was not shown.
  - **Fix:** restore the bullet word for word after passage (e), regenerate the manifest, and recheck that the diff
    against `main` holds the five passages and nothing else.

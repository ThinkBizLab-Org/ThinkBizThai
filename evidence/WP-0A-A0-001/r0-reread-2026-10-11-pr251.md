## 3. Findings at this head

- **R0-251-10 (blocking, new). Q-034-1 reverses an answer the Owner gave on 2026-10-08 without saying so, and the
  "never integrates its own work" rule is left in force without a reading.**
  - **What the Owner already decided.** RFC-2026-030 Q-030-5 put exactly this to him: (a) a run that is not the Author
    presses an M or L merge, or (b) the Author presses, "on recorded Reviewer and Tester verdicts for the exact head",
    "with §2 saying that pressing is not integration and §3.2's verdict named as the integration step". He chose (a).
  - **What still says so after the merge.** RFC-2026-030 §2, first bullet ("The principle, unchanged and absolute"):
    "Every tier below H still has at least one independent reading by a run that is not the Author's, and the merge of
    an M or L PR is pressed by a run that is not the Author's". RFC-2026-034 amends RFC-2026-030 §3 and §3.1–§3.2, not
    §2. The guide keeps "an Author must never approve, test-verify, integrate, or gate-approve their own work" (line 27)
    and "The Author never approves, test-verifies, integrates, or authorizes their own work" (line 74). RFC-2026-030 §3.1
    reads an Author pressing an unread PR as integrating it.
  - **What the RFC says instead.** §2.4 restates the unchanged rule as "the Author never writes another role's
    verdict", which is weaker than the guide's text it claims to keep.
  - **Effect.** With Q-034-1 answered yes, A0 pressing its own fast-lane PR (no reading at all) or risk-lane PR breaks
    RFC-2026-030 §2 and the guide's two sentences as written, and nothing in the repository says otherwise.
  - **Fix:** Q-034-1 says it reverses Q-030-5 (a) and goes further than Q-030-5 (b) (no reading at all on the fast
    lane); the Amends line and §3 add RFC-2026-030 §2 first bullet; §2.2 or §2.3 says, in A0's recommended answer, that
    pressing a risk-lane or fast-lane merge is not integration and the round audit (§2.3) is the integration step;
    §2.4 quotes the guide's rule as it stands. If the Owner chooses the alternative, nothing in §2 changes.

    further PR, while RFC-2026-034 says one more is needed. Two Approved RFCs would disagree on when the G0 exit closes.

    says: "§6 item 2 is read as a PR after the thirteenth freeze that amends the gate text to record the exit closed;
    this PR, though it amends the passage, does not meet it". §2.1 stops presenting that as §6's text.

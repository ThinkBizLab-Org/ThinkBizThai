## 3. New findings

Stop-the-line: **no**. Nothing is merged or in effect.

### Blocking

- **A1-251r2-1. The rework deleted a "Temporary manual merge control" bullet that no passage, no §3 row and no question
  names: the handoff record of each merge and the revert-only rollback.** On the base, the section has five bullets; the
  fifth reads "Record the PR URL, head SHA, CI run, evidence links, and rollback plan in the handoff. Roll back with a
  reviewed revert commit/PR, never a force-push." At the head it is gone (measured: 13 lines removed, 11 of them the five
  passages; `CONTRIBUTING_AGENTS.md` lines 81–82 on the base, now nothing between the new fourth bullet and the closing
  paragraph). RFC §7 (e) replaces "fourth bullet" only; §3 line 129 names "bullets 2 and 4"; §3 line 135 says RFC-2026-002
  "Items 4 and 5 unchanged", and item 5 is this rollback rule; §11 reverts "the five passages", so a rollback of RFC-034
  would not bring the bullet back. The handoff's `acceptance_results[0]` says "nothing else in the file changes": false.
  Why it matters for security: under the risk and fast lanes the integration verdict moves to the round audit (§2.3),
  which reads merged work after the fact from exactly this record (PR URL, head, CI run, evidence links, rollback
  plan); and "never a force-push" for a rollback is the guide's only statement that `main` history is not rewritten to
  undo a bad merge (bullet 1 covers pushing, not rollback). No test pins the text (`git grep`), so CI stays green
  without it. It reads as an editing slip: bullet 4 was two wrapped lines and the replacement took the next two.
  **Fix (text):** restore the bullet verbatim as the fifth bullet, regenerate the manifest; no RFC text changes.

- **A1-251r2-2. The audit pull request gets a lane of its own, with no reader, which is beyond the Owner's option, is
  not a §9 question, and contradicts this RFC's own §2.2 and §3.** §2.3 (lines 104–106): the audit PR "holds only the
  auditors' own files on WP-0A-A0-001's branch; each file is its own reading, so no further reader is needed, and A0
  presses it once CI is green". Those files are the verdicts that move packages past `in_review` (lines 107–109,
  passage (b)), including packages A0 authored. Under RFC-2026-025 §6.1 rule 2 a role's verdict file, or any file in a
  subdirectory of `evidence/<package>/`, is **not** a record, and Q0 measured an `evidence/audits/` commit as tier H and
  not records-only; so by §2.2 (line 76 "anything it cannot place is H"; line 83 "every other tier H … anything
  unplaced") it is a risk-lane PR, and by RFC-2026-025 §6 (lines 134, 140: "§6.1–§6.3 unchanged", "RFC-2026-025 §5
  item 6 and §6" not amended) it is not records. Today such a PR takes at least one independent reading. The new rule
  lets the run whose work is being judged assemble the verdict PR and press it with nobody confirming that each file is
  the auditor's own, unaltered, and that none (A1's finding file, say) was left out; "A1's audit finding of any grade
  blocks those moves" (line 108) then depends on A1's file being there. That is the "Author … authorizes their own
  work" risk that passage (e) and §2.4 line 117 keep forbidden. **Fix (text), either:** (i) the audit PR's commits are
  each a `cherry-pick -x` of the auditor's own commit touching only that auditor's file, the presser records for each
  that the blob equals the auditor's commit, all four files are present, and §3 declares the amendment of RFC-2026-025
  §6.1 for `evidence/audits/**`; or (ii) the audit PR is a records PR with one independent reading (an auditor other
  than the presser confirms the set is complete and unaltered); or (iii) put the presser-alone rule to the Owner as
  Q-034-5.

### Advisory (text measured against the repository; for C0, A1 and R0 to weigh)

- **Q0-251-2.** RFC §1 item 1 says "All fourteen First-Slice contracts are `Candidate`". RFC-2026-031 §2 makes the
  First-Slice set thirteen (CTR-USG-001 is outside it); the catalog lists fourteen, all Candidate. The claim holds for
  the catalog. As written about the First Slice, the count is wrong.
- **Q0-251-3.** RFC §1 item 1 says the old "Current gate constraint" allows implementation "only on `Frozen v1`
  contracts". The text removed from line 23 says nothing about `Frozen`: it says "Until G0 passes, agents may work only
  on spikes, prototypes, fixtures, fake adapters, contract tests, and reversible foundation that does not bind
  production schema or an external provider". It follows register line 373. The new text drops the "does not bind
  production schema or an external provider" limit. Neither §2.4 nor §9 names that. Register line 373 is not named in
  §2.1's override or in "Amends". The Owner confirmed on 2026-10-08 (§10.3) that line 373 binds, and RFC-2026-031 §3.3
  cites it. The risk lane covers migrations and external side effects, but only for review depth. The RFC does not say
  whether line 373 still binds after the merge.
- **Q0-251-4.** RFC §2.1 overrides "the Decision Register lines that tie implementation to `Frozen v1`". Register §5.1
  line 191 ties merging consumers to `Integrated v1` ("merge consumer ได้"). The RFC lets consumers merge against
  `Candidate` and does not address that row.
- **Q0-251-5.** RFC-2026-031 §6 item 2 makes "a PR amending `CONTRIBUTING_AGENTS.md` 'Current gate constraint' is merged"
  one of the two conditions that close the G0 exit. This PR is such a PR. RFC-034 §5 does not say whether its merge
  meets that condition, or whether §6 is amended.
- **Q0-251-6.** §2.2 puts "everything else" in the fast lane. §4 makes fast lane only the tier-H PRs whose sole H
  reasons are an unlisted module, contract text, scripts or test kits. RFC-2026-030 §3's H list has classes that are in
  neither the risk lane nor §4's list: jobs, queues and schedulers; storage and deletion; provider adapters; mail and
  notifications; `package.json`, lockfiles and tool configuration; work-package scope or roles; and a status move past
  `in_review`. By §2.2 they are fast lane. By §4 they are not demoted. The new CONTRIBUTING passage also drops "decided
  by the base's copy of `scripts/db/classify-review-tier.mjs` and never lowered by hand". The RFC states that until §8
  item 2, A0 sets the lane by hand.

None of these is a test failure. Each is a statement in the RFC that I checked against the repository's text. Whether any
of them must change before the Owner merges is for C0, A1 and R0.

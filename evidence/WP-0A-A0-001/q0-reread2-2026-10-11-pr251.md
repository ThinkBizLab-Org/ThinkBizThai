### Advisory

- **Q0-251-13: §2.2's list of the guards CI runs is narrower than what CI runs.** The governance row lists
  `scripts/verify-*.mjs`, `scripts/scan-repository-secrets.mjs`, `scripts/refresh-author-handoff.mjs`,

  principle ("any script or test file CI runs as a guard"; "If it is unclear whether a path is a guard, it is

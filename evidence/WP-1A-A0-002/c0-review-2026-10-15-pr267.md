### C0-267-1 (advisory): item (4)(b) is held as a text test with a stand-in parser, not the CLI control Q0 asked for

Q0's "Wanted" for (b) (`q0-reread5` §7) was "a CLI control whose readers disagree", with `next.config.mjs` holding
`import type` and a hashbang next.config as the two directions. The PR instead extracts `parsedShape` from the CLI's
text and runs it with a stand-in parser whose two readings were measured once with typescript 7.0.2 on this branch.

`scripts/verify-app-boundaries.mjs`. The consequence, disclosed in the handoff's `known_limitations[0]`: whether the
real parser still reads those two files differently from the text reader is held by no suite or CI step. The test is
fail-closed against a rename or rewiring of `parsedShape` (it pins three exact lines and the function header). Whether
this closes Q0-257r6-1 (b) is Q0's to write.

### C0-267-2 (advisory): the Q0-259-1 test also pins `exclude` to today's exact list

`assert.deepEqual(project.exclude, ['node_modules', '**/node_modules', 'apps/**/.next', 'test-kits/**'])` is a
positive-control precondition, but it means any future `exclude` entry, even one that spares `src/`, fails the test.
That is slightly more than the item ("pin src/**") and makes the guard stricter, not weaker; a `tsconfig.json` edit is
governance read by all four roles anyway. No change asked.

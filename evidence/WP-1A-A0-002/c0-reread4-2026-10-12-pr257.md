- The handoff's `tests[]` entry for `verify-app-build-graph.mjs` (line 153) still ends "nothing outside apps/web/**".
  The check also admits `src/modules/**` and reachable packages, as `known_limitations[0]` on the same head says
  correctly. A fix confined to the handoff may ride under carry clause (c).
- The tracked-`.env` refusal covers `apps/**`, `src/**` and the root, "because Next.js loads one into the build" (RFC
  paragraph, code comment). `@next/env` 16.3.8 loads `.env.$mode.local`, `.env.local` (not in test mode),
  `.env.$mode` and `.env` from the directory Next is given, which is `apps/web`. A root or `src/` `.env` is not
  loaded by `next build` today. So the refusal reaches further than its reason. That is stricter, not looser, and
  harmless. A `.env` under `db/` or elsewhere passes [measured], which matches the text. No change is needed. I record
  it so that a later reader does not take the root case as a measured route.

Accuracy points I checked and found sound:

- The CI comment says "Next.js adopts a key from a `.env` file only when the environment does not define it". In
  `@next/env` 16.3.8 `dist/index.js`, a parsed key is copied only when `typeof <original env>[key] === "undefined"`. An
  empty string is defined, so `NODE_OPTIONS: ''` at job level keeps a `.env`'s `NODE_OPTIONS` out, provided the runner
  exports the empty value. I did not measure the runner. A1 and Q0 own CI. No step in `ci.yml` sets `NODE_OPTIONS`
  again (`grep`: lines 1309–1315 and 1364 only).
- The graph check's new "WHAT IT DOES NOT SEE" paragraph, the CI step comment, the RFC paragraph and the handoff's
  `known_limitations[0..1]` all now say that code which runs during the build is held by the shapes and refusals, not by
  the graph check. This removes the round-three over-claim that A1-257-8/-9 turned on.
- The text reader's `//` comment now ends at LF, CR, U+2028 and U+2029. Measured: in `apps/web/next.config.ts`, a

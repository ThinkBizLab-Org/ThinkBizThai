## 5. Advisory

**A1-267-1 (advisory, not a finding against this PR; no grade; non-blocking).** Test (b) holds the CLI's three uses of
`parsedShape` by `cli.includes(<line>)`, so a copy of a pinned line in a comment satisfies the pin while the code
beside it reads with one reader. P3 shows it for the alias controls: with `readConfigShape` in place of
`parsedShape(parser, configs)` and the original call kept as a comment, every app suite passes. The effect would be that
the CLI's alias controls exercise the text reader only; the repository's own configurations still go through
`parsedShape`, and the evasion needs a deliberate edit to `scripts/verify-app-boundaries.mjs`, a governance path that
itself takes the four role readings. This PR lowers nothing; it adds a pin that is narrower than its name. A later
increment could strip comments before matching, or count the call sites in code. The disposition of this advisory is
A0's; it does not hold the press.

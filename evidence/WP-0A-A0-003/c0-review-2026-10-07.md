  has` runs every credential decoy, every non-exempt PII decoy and the email exemption at every real file
  path, at `<dir>/new-file.txt` for every real directory, and at a new top-level directory. The test
  writes the ignore list and the prose prefixes itself and requires them to equal the scanner's, so
  widening the scanner's list fails before anything is scanned.
- **B. Size and offset independence.** Offsets 0/1/4/8/64/512 KiB × head/middle/tail. Every decoy past
  9 KiB through `scanText` (per-rule truncation). A file of exactly `MAX_FILE_BYTES` (8 MiB) with the

  further: `scanTree` returns `{findings, files, bytes}`. The read list must equal an independent walk
  (paths and total bytes), and a credential appended to a copy of **every** repository file, plus 13
  not-yet-existing paths, must be reported at that path. A scanner that claims a file as read without
  matching it is caught by the copy test (C4b, M26b, X7 below).

`scanDirectory` keeps its signature and return shape (`scanTree(...).findings`), so the export used by
the CLI, the ratchet and other suites is unchanged. `scanTree` is an additive export. No contract in

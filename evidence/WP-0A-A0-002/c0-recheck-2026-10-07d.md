**Why the over-approximation is safe.** The condition counts every non-ASCII, non-whitespace code unit as a word
character. Outside strings, comments, templates and regex literals, the only such characters V8 accepts are
identifier characters (including ZWNJ/ZWJ and surrogate halves of astral identifiers). Any other non-ASCII
character there is a syntax error, so the module cannot load and no payload runs. `trim()` strips exactly the JS
WhiteSpace and LineTerminator sets, including U+FEFF, so a non-ASCII space still ends a word (NBSP rows above).

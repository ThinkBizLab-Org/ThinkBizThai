### N2: Discover is not pinned (Minor)

`601111111111111`. Prefix `6011` also matches RuPay's `/^(60|81|82)/` at length 16. I narrowed Discover's

does not block the merge. It is owed to Q0's domain in this package's next increment, or in this pass if A0
touches the test again.

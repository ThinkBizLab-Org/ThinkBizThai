## 4. Change classification (RFC-2026-031 §3.3)

**Classification: breaking (narrowing).** RFC-2026-031 §3.3 defines breaking as "any change to the set of documents
the contract's `schema.json` accepts", including narrowing. This change narrows CTR-OBS-001. A document whose
`module.implementation_version` has 65 or more code points was valid at `9d0b3d23` and is invalid at `c202e964`.

- **No widening.** Every document valid after the change was valid before.
- **No change in meaning.** The meaning of `module.implementation_version` does not change.
- **No metadata change.** `owner`, `consumers` and the index's `required_before_freeze` do not change.
- **No gap is removed.** The new fixture and the new `x-bound-note` are non-breaking on their own (an `x-`
  annotation, and a fixture that confirms the new behaviour).

**What binds, and what does not.**
- CTR-OBS-001 is `Draft` (manifest `"status": "Draft"`, version `1.0.0`, unchanged).
- §3.3's "a breaking change needs an RFC" and the `2.0.0` rule apply after `Frozen`. They do not bind this PR. The
  RFC's own consequence paragraph says a gap closed before the freeze needs none.
- `version` stays `1.0.0`, which is correct at Draft.
- What does bind at Draft is the co-owner signature on the value (A6: given, §2 above) and the full C0, A1, Q0 and
  R0 round. `open_blockers[20]` returns the package to `in_review` for that round.

**Effect on real documents.** None of the shipped `valid-*` fixtures is affected (`1.4.0`, `0.9.2`). The new bound
equals CTR-EVT-001's, so after this change a health signal accepts exactly the version strings an event envelope
does.

**Note on authority.** RFC-2026-031 is Approved by the Owner at `e2ec50a8`, but it is not yet on `main`; it is on
PR #229. I apply §3.3 as the instruction asks. The classification does not depend on that PR merging: under any
reading, the change is a narrowing.

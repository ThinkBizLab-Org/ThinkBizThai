## 2. Findings

Stop-the-line: **no.**

### A1-239-1 — The §9 answers rest on a general delegation where the press condition names the Owner (blocking for a delegated press until C-1 and C-2 below are met; no change to this head is required)

- **What the night delegation's words do.** "ผมให้สิทธิคุณทั้งหมด ไม่ต้องขออนุญาต" is very broad. A0's reading is
  narrower than the words, not wider:
  - A0 picks only its own stated recommendation.
  - The Owner may revisit any answer.
  - No role, the four-role requirement, any stop-the-line or CI is lifted.
  - Nothing is moved to `done` and nothing is frozen.
  - The delegation ends when the Owner next speaks.

  On the night words alone, the reading does not widen authority.
- **Where the reading falls short.** The press authority for this PR is item 3 of the three-governance-PRs disposition.
  - That exception to RFC-2026-025 §5 item 6 is valid: the PR is named by content, after the question was put.
  - The option text the Owner chose sets its own condition: "และ (สำหรับ RFC) คุณตอบคำถามใน RFC แล้ว". The "คุณ" in it is
    the Owner.
  - A0's transcription of that disposition, on `main`, says the direction "supplies no … his answers to the questions in
    RFCs (2) and (3)".
  - For RFC-2026-031 and RFC-2026-032 the Owner answered personally.
  - Here a later, general grant ("full authority") is used to meet an earlier, specific condition that names the Owner
    himself. The result is that A0 answers the Owner's questions on a governance PR and then presses that same PR.
- **Why the merge record does not cover it.** The new disposition file does not name that condition, and does not say
  that the night words are being read as discharging it. It lists the OBS RFC only as "already directed". Nor does the
  record say that A0 disclosed this exact use to the Owner at `17:10:42Z` and got no reply.
- **The cost question.** Q-033-3 is the cost question. RFC §5 itself reserves any budget increase to the Owner
  "because it is cost". The total cost of accepting the ceiling depends on the number of metric names (A1-239-3), and
  the RFC does not bound that number.
- **Why this does not block the text.** Nothing in §2–§8 binds until the RFC is Accepted, and nothing binds in the
  contract until the §8 increment. Every answer is revisable. The narrowing reduces risk.
- **What it does block.** A delegated press may not rely on the reading without a bound in time, and without the record
  saying what it is standing in for. Conditions C-1 and C-2 resolve this.

### A1-239-2 — The §6.2 runtime check bounds the count of `module_key`/`capability_key` values, not their content (advisory for this head; a condition on the §8 increment, C-3)

- Both labels keep the pattern `^[a-z0-9_.:-]+$` up to 64 characters. A lower-case UUID, a workspace id, or a
  composite like `export.ws_3f2a…` satisfies it.
- §6.2's "population check" asks only for at most 32 modules and at most 16 capabilities per module. A module could
  spend one of its 16 slots on a tenant- or user-specific key and stay inside the count. That is within budget for
  cardinality, but it puts an identifier in a label. §4.2 forbids that by intent, but the check would not catch it.
- The CTR-OBS-001 manifest already records that nothing checks that `sli_tags.capability_key` appears in
  `readiness.capabilities`. A6's dictionary already states the `module_key` check as "a check against the module
  ownership registry", which is a membership check.
- The runtime gap that §6.2 declares should therefore be a membership check:
  - `module_key` ∈ the registered CTR-MOD-001 module keys;
  - `capability_key` ∈ that module's declared capabilities.

  Membership bounds content as well as count. The count follows from it.

### A1-239-3 — The cost ceiling is per metric name; the number of metric names is not bounded (advisory)

- §4.1 and Q-033-3 give 24,480 series per metric per environment.
- Total series, which is what a metric backend charges for and what a label-explosion DoS exhausts, is that figure
  multiplied by the number of metric names. Neither this RFC nor CTR-OBS-001 bounds that number.
- When the Owner revisits Q-033-3, the question should say "per metric name". The metric-name set, or its budget,
  should be named as owed (A6) before any real backend is connected.

### A1-239-4 — "with the lists of §2 and §3" uses the `outcome` budget, not its list (advisory)

- 24,480 counts `outcome` as 5 (budget 4 + absent). With the §3 seed of 3 the factor is 4, giving 19,584.
- The stated figure is the more conservative of the two, so no residual risk is understated.
- The wording should say "with the §2 list and the §3 budget".

### A1-239-5 — The delegation timestamp is A0's clock reading, 6 s after the words (advisory)

- The disposition says the words were "said … at 2026-10-09T17:10:06Z".
- The transcript has the Owner's message at `17:10:00.486Z`. `17:10:06Z` is the output of A0's `date -u` call at
  `17:10:06.457Z`.
- Nothing turns on the 6 s. It can be corrected in any later edit of that file, or noted in the post-merge record.

### Interim risk until §8 lands (stated, not a finding)

- **The restriction.** "No consumer built under this contract may emit `sli_tags` to a real metric backend before that
  budget lands" is on `main` in the CTR-OBS-001 manifest `untestable_by_schema`. RFC §6.2 and A6's dictionary repeat
  it, and this PR keeps it. It is prose, not a test. Today it is sufficient because no emitter and no backend exist
  (measured above).
- **The label vector this RFC closes.** The accepted-gap value shows correlation-id and attempt-number material in
  `error_code`. That vector stays open on `main` until §8 merges. It is reachable only through the restriction above.
- **What does not change.** Accepting this RFC changes nothing in the contract. The residual risk is the same before
  and after this merge, until §8.
- **Authority.** The RFC widens no one's authority:
  - adding an `outcome` value needs A6 plus a four-role round, inside a budget of 4;
  - adding an `error_code` value is an ERR change, capped at 64 by §6.2 item 2's test;
  - any budget increase returns to the Owner.

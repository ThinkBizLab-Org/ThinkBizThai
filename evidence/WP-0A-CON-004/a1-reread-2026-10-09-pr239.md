- **`outcome` gains `error`.** It is a closed word, not an identifier. The series ceiling already counted `outcome` at 5
  (budget 4 + absent), so 24,480 is unchanged.
- **The budget of 4 is now full.** Any further `outcome` value is a budget increase, so it returns to the Owner under
  §5, where before A6 alone could add one.
- **`error_code`.** "8 enforced, cap 64" makes any value above 8 an Owner question (§2 "Budget in force"). Before, the
  ceiling of 64 read as headroom.

    shipper, not of a document.

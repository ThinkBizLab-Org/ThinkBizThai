**The precedent stays refused.** §1 records the refusal. §8 lists "A1's refusal of the precedent, which this RFC
implements and does not replace" under *not changed*. Nothing in the PR ratifies, after the fact, a single-owner
contract fixing a jointly owned contract's syntax. I still refuse it, and this RFC is consistent with that.

**Is "equal accept sets shown by probe" what §5 delivers?** Yes, and it delivers more than I asked for.
- Assertion 1 is the real guarantee: identical non-`x-` keywords, validated by one validator, give identical accept sets.
- Assertion 2 checks what keyword identity alone does not: sibling rules in the two whole documents.
- Assertion 3 stops both sides drifting together without a visible edit.

My probe shows the corpus separates the tree from the §8 state exactly. There are 4 divergent values before the edit
and none after, and identity goes from false to true.

**Is the corpus adequate?** Yes. It contains every value that diverges today, both sides of the 128 boundary, my full
Probe 2 and Probe 4, and the encoding edge cases where a JavaScript regex and the specification could disagree
(trailing `\n`, astral, non-ASCII). The RFC says "at least". NUL and a leading `\n` are cheap additions for the §8 author,
but they are not needed for equality: both are already rejected identically in my probe.

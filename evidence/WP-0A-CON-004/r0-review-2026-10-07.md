CTR-MOD-001 (Candidate, A0) `module_key`, `capabilities[].capability_key` and `dependencies[].module_key`
have no `maxLength` (`KNOWN_UNBOUNDED`, owed by WP-0A-CON-003). After this PR, CTR-OBS-001 `module.module_key`,
readiness `capability_key`, `dependency_key`, both `sli_tags` keys and CTR-SEC-001 `scope.capability_key` cap
those same keys at 64, as CTR-EVT-001 `producer.module_key` already did. A module key of 65+ characters is a
valid CTR-MOD-001 manifest today and could not report health, carry an SLI tag or scope a credential. No such
key exists in any fixture, and the asymmetry predates this PR (RFC-2026-009). What follows from it: when
WP-0A-CON-003 bounds CTR-MOD-001 through its Candidate change path, the bound must be 64 or less, or these
values move with it. The SEC `scope.capability_key` reason cites a downstream label rather than the
registry that issues capability keys; C0 may want that noted. Not a merge condition.

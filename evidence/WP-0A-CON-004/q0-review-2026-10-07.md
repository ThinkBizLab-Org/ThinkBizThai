### 4.3 Each new fixture is killed by its bound and by nothing else

For each of the 22 `invalid-*-too-long.json` fixtures I validated against the shipped schema, then deleted
**every** `maxLength` site in that schema one at a time and re-validated:

- each fixture is rejected with exactly one error, `<path>: longer than maxLength N`;
- the offending value is exactly `N + 1` characters long and is the longest string in the fixture;
- exactly **one** deletion turns it accepted, and that site is the field the fixture is named for.

22 of 22 pass all three. So each fixture is a single-site killer for its own bound.

Boundary probe (independent of the fixtures): taking a shipped valid fixture and setting a field to exactly
`N` characters gives 0 errors, `N + 1` gives 1 error, for `scope.workspace_id`, `scope.capability_key`,
`correlation_id` and `revocation.actor.id` / `revocation.reason_key` (SEC), `audit_id` and `actor.id`
(AUD), `module.module_key`, `sli_tags.module_key`, `sli_tags.capability_key` and `correlation.trace_id`
(OBS). The bounds are inclusive at the stated value.

### 4.4 The `sli_tags` pattern rewrite keeps its accept set

`^[a-z0-9_.:-]{1,64}$` became `^[a-z0-9_.:-]+$` plus `maxLength: 64` on `sli_tags.module_key` and
`sli_tags.capability_key`. 1,600 random strings over an alphabet including in-class, out-of-class and
space characters at lengths 0, 1, 2, 63, 64, 65, 66 and 200, plus `''`, `'a'×64`, `'a'×65` and a
non-ASCII string: **0 disagreements** between the old regex and the new schema. Because the pattern admits
ASCII only, code-point and UTF-16 length cannot differ on any accepted string.

The existing `invalid-sli-tags-{module,capability}-key-pattern.json` fixtures carry 23-character values,
are rejected by the shipped schema, and are accepted with the pattern deleted. So the rewrite does not
leave the pattern site to be killed by length; it is still killed by shape.

`dependencies.module_key` are still in `KNOWN_UNBOUNDED` (owed by WP-0A-CON-003). CTR-SEC-001 and

credential or reported in readiness. Recording it so that package picks 64 (or records why not).

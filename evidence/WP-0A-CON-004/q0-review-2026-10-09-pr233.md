## 5. Findings

- **None blocking.**
- **Q-A1 (advisory, for CON-008).** `mustNotice`, the JSON-reversal helper in `ratchets-bite.test.mjs`, has no no-op
  guard. `mustNoticeSourceEdit` has one (`…the edit would be a no-op`). When the target contract moves, a stale reversal
  therefore fails as "must notice … (status 0)", which reads like a broken guard rather than a stale probe. That is
  what happened to the CTR-SEC-001 reversal here, and it will happen to CTR-NTF-001 when A5 promotes it. A pre-check
  that the mutated document differs from the original would give the right reason. This PR is not the place for it.
- **Q-A2 (advisory, test behaviour only).** The `ratchets-bite.test.mjs` repoint keeps the reversal's meaning (a Draft
  promoted by drift) and bites for the census reason. From the test side, it is the minimal edit the status move
  forces. Whether it falls within "the census assertions" of A1 K1 and A6 §8 is for A1, A6 and R0 to rule, not for me.

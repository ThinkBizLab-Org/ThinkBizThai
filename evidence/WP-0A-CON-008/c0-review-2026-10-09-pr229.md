## 3. Soundness of the parts asked about

- **The `declared_gaps` shape (§5.2): sound and implementable.**
  - Each existing field fits only part of the need: `accepted_gaps` keys must be accepted fixtures, `untestable_by_*`
    are prose pinned by digest, and `MANIFEST_KEYS` rejects any key not on it. A new key is therefore required, and
    the RFC's reasons are accurate.
  - The closed lists, the `source` forms and the mechanical coverage rule can be checked by a test.
  - The `freeze_boundary` and `open_blockers` items are left to C0 to match by reading. That is honest, because no
    script can split prose into items.
- **The undeclarable kinds (§5.5): sound in principle, with one consequence the RFC does not address.** See A-1.
- **The breaking-change definition (§3.3): sound and strict.**
  - Counting widening as breaking is right for a security contract.
  - "Doubt → breaking" with C0 classifying in writing can be implemented.
  - It is strict in two places, which are recorded here and are not defects:
    - adding a consumer category to the index counts as breaking;
    - closing a gap by narrowing the schema after the freeze needs an RFC and `2.0.0`. §3.3's "Consequence" bullet
      rightly says to close the bounded-value items before the freeze.
- **The freeze review (§4): consistent with itself.** §4.1 items 2 and 6 restate §5.5 items 1 and 2. The §4.3 role
  checks match §4.5, where everything moves in one commit, and §7.2.
- **The exit condition (§6): sound.** The Owner has now confirmed three conditions, not two (A-3).
- **The test-change spec (§7): implementable as written.**
  - The ratchet trap it names is real. Once a contract is pinned `Frozen`, the existing `status = 'Frozen'` reversal
    becomes a no-op for it.
  - Two details need settling in the §7.1 PR (A-4 and A-6).

**Advisory:**

- **A-1. §5.5 (3) and CTR-SEC-001's recorded scope-binding gap. This must be settled before CTR-SEC-001's freeze review
  opens.**
  - CTR-SEC-001 `untestable_by_schema` (2), "SCOPE BINDING", says this: "a document, or a CTR-MOD-001 manifest listing
    the same handle, may claim another tenant's scope and be accepted". It belongs to the PT-010 resolver, and JSON
    Schema cannot express it.
  - That is "a document that could cross a tenant boundary", so §5.5 (3) makes it undeclarable.
  - The schema cannot close it. Closing it with evidence on `main` needs the resolver or an equivalent runtime check,
    and before the G0 exit the interim rule (register line 373) admits only "reversible foundation".
  - CTR-SEC-001 is in the First Slice, so as written §5.5 (3) may make §6's first condition unreachable. The RFC does
    not say what evidence closes a tenant gap that only runtime can enforce. §8 does not list that work.
  - The Owner was not shown this consequence when he answered Q-031-3.
  - Remedy, for A0 with A1: before the SEC freeze review, put a clarifying amendment to the Owner (for example, what
    evidence counts as closing a runtime-enforced tenant binding). Alternatively, record what foundation work closes
    it.
  - It does not block this PR, because nothing freezes on merge, and §7.1 must land first in any case.
  - CTR-USG-001 carries a similar cross-tenant attribution gap. That does not matter here, because USG needs only
    Candidate.
- **A-2. §9 and §4.4 are overtaken by §10 on who presses.**
  - §9 says the RFC "grants no exception to RFC-2026-025 §5 item 6 beyond the Owner's standing direction already
    transcribed in the 2026-10-08 disposition §10.2". §4.4 calls the press reading "A0's".
  - The appended §10 and disposition §8.3 record that the Owner's Q-031-4 answer is itself an exception for #229 and
    for freeze PRs.
  - This is not a contradiction of substance. The exception is the Owner's, not the RFC's. But a reader of §9 alone is
    misled.
  - The approved text cannot be edited. The records PR after the merge should say that §10 governs on the press.
- **A-3. §6 says the exit closes when "both" conditions hold.**
  - The "one more condition" (USG reaching Candidate) was a question, and Q-031-1 has answered it.
  - The closing record of the exit must check three conditions.
- **A-4. The 80-character floor is off by one.**
  - §5.2 says `gap` must be "at least 80 characters", and that "the existing `accepted_gaps` reason test sets that
    floor".
  - That test asserts `reason.length > 80` (`shared-kernel-schema-conformance.test.mjs`), which is at least 81.
  - The §7.1 PR should pick one and say which.
- **A-5. §4.1 item 2 puts "presence, not sufficiency" in quotation marks as RFC-2026-010's words.**
  - The phrase does not occur in RFC-2026-010. Its wording is "presence of the artifact the phrase names … not proof
    that the artifact satisfies the intent behind the phrase" (lines 54–61).
  - The substance is right. The quotation marks are not.
- **A-6. The §7.2 census list is incomplete.**
  - It omits `shared-kernel-envelope-contracts.test.mjs` line 150, which asserts `'Candidate'` per id for the envelope
    contracts.
  - The first API, PAG or IDM freeze will meet it.
- **A-7. The work-package prose is stale at the head.**
  - `work-packages/WP-0A-CON-008.json` was written at `d0c92447` and still reads "Proposed" in three places:
    - `scope.include`;
    - the `[6]` append ("RFC-2026-031, status Proposed");
    - `[8]` ("status Proposed", and "OWED … (1) Owner: answers to Q-031-1 to Q-031-5").
  - Each was true when written and is false at the head. The handoff is current.
  - The records PR after the merge should append the Approved fact and the discharge of `[8]` (1) to `[8]` (append
    only), and may correct `scope.include`.
- **A-8. Three rules are not specified, and should be settled in the §7.1 PR or by amendment before the first
  freeze.**
  - **Re-signing after the freeze.** §3.2 says "A signature on an earlier text does not carry to a changed text", but

  - **Composition.** When a contract it composes takes a breaking change (§4.2 "is affected"), what must the composing
    contract do?
  - **Gap ids.** "Never reused" cannot be checked mechanically once §5.4 removes a closed entry.

  - Read it with §3.2 item 2 and §4.3: for CTR-NTF-001 it is A5's signature as owner. The eight contracts A0 owns alone

## 3. Findings

### N-1 (Low–Medium, record; governance, not security) — the Candidate → Frozen ordering in §1/§4 is circular against the G0 Pass Rule

The record says "**No contract can be Frozen before that record [WP-0A-A0-010] merges**" and, in
§4 item 3, orders the work as "`WP-0A-A0-010` (G0 exit) merged first; then an RFC that defines the
freeze review". But the same Decision Register, §7.2 G0 Pass Rule item 2 (`:365`), makes freeze
a **precondition** of G0: "Contract ที่ First Slice ใช้ต้อง `Frozen v1`". Read together, `:43`
("Frozen v1 = passed G0") and `:365` (G0 needs First-Slice contracts Frozen) describe one event —
the freeze review is part of the G0 exit — not two in sequence. Following the record's §4 literally,
G0 cannot exit (its item 2 is unmet) and no contract can freeze (G0 has not exited).

Consequence for this PR: none of its verdicts changes — every Candidate → Frozen step is still
blocked, and its per-contract lists are still what stands in the way. Consequence for the next
step: whoever writes `WP-0A-A0-010` or the freeze-review RFC should read §7.2(2) as making the
freeze of First-Slice contracts part of the G0 exit, not something after it. **Fix (A0):** amend
§1 and §4 item 3 to cite §7.2(2) and state the two as one gate, or record the circularity as an
open item for the Product Owner. Does not block this merge; nothing moved on the circular reading.

### N-2 (Low, record; security-adjacent) — a recorded secret-scanner hazard is deleted from the only place it lived

The handoff at `bd019c9c` carried, in `open_risks_or_blockers`, the diagnosis that a SHA-256 digest
in `test-kits/integrity-manifest.json` can by chance contain a Luhn-valid 13–19 digit run, that
`scripts/scan-repository-secrets.mjs` then correctly reports a card number with no allowlist by
design, and that the earlier branch rename was "a workaround and not a fix". This PR's handoff drops
that entry. I searched `evidence/`, `work-packages/`, `handoffs/`, `docs/` and `scripts/` at head:
the diagnosis now exists **nowhere** on the branch. The done list does not mention removing it.

The hazard is unchanged — the scanner and the regenerate-on-every-change manifest are as they
were, and this very increment regenerated a digest line. Losing the record means the next author
who trips it meets "a mystery, not a diagnosis", which was the entry's stated purpose. It is not a
secret leak and not a weakening of the scanner, so it is Low. **Fix (A0):** restore the entry to
the handoff, or move it into the manifest's `open_blockers` (where the guard will mirror it), with
an owner — the scanner's owner, since an exemption there is reserved for a reviewed decision.
Does not block the merge if carried as a condition; preferably restored in the next refresh of this
PR.

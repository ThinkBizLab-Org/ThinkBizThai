## 2. Findings

| ID | Grade | Finding |
|---|---|---|
| F-1 | **Medium, condition** | **The common Candidate → Frozen blocker, as written, is a deadlock with the G0 pass rule, and the record does not say so.** §1 reads Register §1.2 ("Frozen v1 = Contract ผ่าน G0") and §5.1 as "G0 must have passed first", concludes "No contract can be Frozen before that record [`WP-0A-A0-010`] merges", and §4 item 3 orders "`WP-0A-A0-010` (G0 exit) merged first; then an RFC that defines the freeze review". But Register §7.2, the G0 Pass Rule, item 2, reads: "Shared contracts เป็นอย่างน้อย `Candidate v1`; Contract ที่ First Slice ใช้ต้อง `Frozen v1`". G0 cannot pass until the First Slice contracts are Frozen, and on the record's reading they cannot be Frozen until G0 has passed. The other reading of "ผ่าน G0" — the freeze review is passed *as part of* G0, so freezing is a G0 deliverable rather than a G0 consequence — removes the cycle and is the one §7.2 (2) requires. The plan's `WP-0A-A0-010` is a *conditional* exit record; whether a conditional exit waives §7.2 (2) is not decided anywhere on `main`. The result ("none moves") does not change, because every contract has its own blockers. The routing does: §4 sends G1 to wait on a record that, by §7.2 (2), itself waits on this package. **Condition before merge:** quote §7.2 (2) beside §1.2/§5.1, state the cycle, and route the reading to the Product Owner + A0 as a gate-rule question (CONTRIBUTING_AGENTS.md sends a gate-rule change through an RFC), instead of asserting an order. |

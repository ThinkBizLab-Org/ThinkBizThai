## 2. Security reading

### 2.1 Can a security-relevant gap be frozen as declared?

§5.5 makes three classes undeclarable (Owner: `รับทั้ง 3 ข้อ (Recommended)`): a missing `required_before_freeze`
item, an open A1 finding of any grade, and a tenant-isolation gap. On its own list that does not name every
security class: secret exposure, audit integrity and personal data in telemetry are not listed. **It is enough,
because of how the rule is built, not because of the list:**

- §4.3 requires an A1 security review of **every** First-Slice contract, and an A1 finding of any grade blocks the
  freeze (§4.1 (6), §4.3). §5.5 (2) makes that finding undeclarable. So any gap A1 grades as a finding cannot be
  frozen as declared, whatever its `kind`, `owner` or `closes_before`.
- The breaking-change definition (§3.3) counts **widening** the accepted set as breaking, and removing a declared gap
  without its closing evidence as breaking. After a freeze, nobody can relax a security bound or quietly drop a gap
  without an RFC.
- `declared_gaps` is pinned in both directions (§7.1, `DECLARED_GAP_DIGESTS`), so a gap cannot be reworded or deleted
  without a reviewed edit.

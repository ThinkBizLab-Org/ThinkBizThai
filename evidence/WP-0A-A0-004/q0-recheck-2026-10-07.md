## 6. Findings

**Q4 (Low, test coverage).** The `&` half of the summary escaping is unpinned: N16 survives because the test's
`<&>` lines still lose their `<` and `>`. Effect if it regressed: a path holding `&lt;b&gt;` would render as the text
`<b>` in the summary (entities decode to text, not markup, since `<` and `>` stay escaped). Cosmetic; no injection. One
assertion closes it (an `&` in the output appears as `&amp;`).

**Q5 (Info, robustness).** The resume line relies on the classifier output ending with a newline. A stub whose output
has no trailing newline makes `sed` (BSD here; GNU does the same) leave the last line open, so `::<token>::` is glued to
it and is not a resume line; my parser ends the step still stopped. The failure direction is inert (fewer commands, not
more), the step prints nothing after that line except via the summary file, and the base classifier on `main` always
ends its output with a newline (`console.log`, Node's crash trace), so a pull request cannot cause it short of the
classifier being killed mid-write. Whether a stop window left open outlives the step on the runner I did not measure.
`printf '\n'` before the resume when the file does not end in one would close it.

**Q6 (Info).** Closure note §5 says 13 scope paths; the head has 14 (the note itself). Not a defect.

**Q7 (Info, reading rule).** `check:handoff` on the pushed head reads green although the cited head is `0f37aa37` and
`61f14919` changes `ci.yml` after it: the guard compares the cited head with `HEAD^` by design, assuming HEAD is the
refresh commit. A green `check:handoff` on this head therefore says nothing about the handoff; the throwaway refresh in §2
is the measure, and the refresh is still owed.

No Blocker or Major finding, and no stop-the-line.

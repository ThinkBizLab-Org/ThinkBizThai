### 2.2 The dash trick, once more

`keyLines` (this PR's code, added in `787ee93f`; not on `d1057429`) skips every line deeper than a line ending in
`:\s*[|>][-+0-9]*\s*$`, taking it for a block-scalar header. It does not strip a comment first, so a comment ending in
`:|` or `:>` opens a "block". On a step written `- # :|` with its keys at column 10, every key is deeper than the dash
line and is skipped: the condition reading and `YAML_FORMS` see only `# :|`.

| # | Mutation on "Type-check negative control" | PyYAML reads | app-stack |
|---|---|---|---|
| U1 | `- # :|`, then `if: always()`, `name: …`, `run: …` at column 10 (round two's S11) | step `if: always()` | 20/21: refused **only by the step pins** ("CI runs the application checks in a job of their own…"), because the step's `name:` and `run:` are not at column 8; the condition test passes |
| **U2** | U1, plus `env:` / `X: "a` and, at column 8 inside that double-quoted scalar, `name: Type-check negative control` and `run: node scripts/verify-typecheck-control.mjs`, closed by `b"` at column 12 | step **`if: always()`**, `name` and `run` as before, `env.X` a string | **21/21 pass** |
| **U3** | U1, plus `env: {X: "1",` and, at column 8 inside that flow mapping, `name: …`, `, # :|`, `run: …`, `, # :|`, closed below (each `, # :|` re-opens the "block", so the closing `}` is not read) | step **`if: always()`**, `name` and `run` as before | **21/21 pass** |

U2 and U3 need a parser that accepts the decoy lines at column 8 inside a scalar or flow mapping that belongs to a mapping
at column 10. YAML 1.2 requires those continuation lines to be indented more than the parent block, so a conforming parser
rejects both, and the plain form (U1) is caught by the pins. PyYAML accepts both, as libyaml does, since neither checks
indentation inside a quoted scalar or a flow collection; whether GitHub's workflow parser accepts them is not measured (that
needs a run on GitHub, and I push nothing). This is the same evidence standard as round two's S3, which was conforming.

So the condition reading still does not fail closed: whatever lies below a comment that ends in `:|` or `:>` is not read,
and only the step pins, or a lenient-parser gap, stand between a condition and the package-code steps. The application job
on the head has no comment on any key line (`grep` for a non-full-line `#` after line 1305 returns nothing), so refusing
one costs nothing.

comment removed (a `#` after whitespace, outside quotes), so `# :|` opens nothing; and, failing closed, refuse within the
application job any key line or dash line that carries a comment, and any dash line with no key on it (the job has
neither). Add U1, U2 and U3 as negative controls on a step no fixture anchors on.

## 4. The finding: a client can set `workspaces.lifecycle_state`

Recorded as `open_blockers[195]` (1). The drafting record found it (§4 there) and left the blocker to A0.

- `010_identity.sql:403`: `grant update (name, lifecycle_state, updated_by) on app.workspaces to authenticated;`
- `workspaces_update_owner` (`:497`): `USING` requires `lifecycle_state in ('active', 'closing')` and an active
  owner; `WITH CHECK` (`:509-517`) requires only the active owner. It does not bound the new state.
- The CHECK at `:156-157` admits eight states. `pinned-grants.json:221` pins the grant. No later migration
  revokes the column or adds a transition guard; the only other policy on the table is `105`'s
  `updated_by` closure.

**Grade: MEDIUM** (A0's). An owner skips §11.4's step-up, the recovery window (`DATA-DEC-04`) and audit
(none can be written today, `[21]`). Today the workspace disappears from its members, and the owner cannot
update it back. No purge job exists, so nothing is deleted. After RFC-2026-027's gate, `access_blocked` set
this way would lock every member out of every family.

**Stop-the-line: A0 reads NOT.** No deletion path exists (no purge job; the deletion-manifest tables are
`[150]`), the actor can only be the workspace's own active owner acting on their own workspace, and no other
tenant is reached. **The reviewers are asked to judge this, not to take it.**

**Owed to:** RFC-2026-027's forward migration (batch 170; A0 author, A1 review): revoke the client `UPDATE`
of `lifecycle_state` and move `pinned-grants.json:221` in the same diff (Q-026-5, Q-027-5; recommendation:
revoke).

**Changed in the evidence commit, after `31ffab4` (one sentence of `[195]`):** the packaging commit's text
said the move was possible by a plain update. On re-reading, PostgreSQL applies a table's SELECT policies
to an UPDATE that reads a column (a `WHERE` or `RETURNING`), and A0 expects, **without measuring**, that this
also checks the new row. If so, `... where id = ...` is refused by `workspaces_select_active_member`, and only
an UPDATE that reads no column succeeds; that one moves **every** workspace the caller owns. Either way the
state is reachable. The entry now says this and asks the reviewers to measure it first. No database was
started for this batch, because nothing the DB layer reads changed. The RFC texts (RFC-026 Q-026-5,
RFC-027 Q-027-5) keep the draft's unqualified wording; they are Proposed and may be corrected in review.

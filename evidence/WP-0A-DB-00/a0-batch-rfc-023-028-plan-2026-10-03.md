   negative control in §6. Owed to the batch that lands shape B, with a `superseded.json` entry for `171`'s
   block (it holds `app_authz` to two policies and six columns). Read, not executed.
2. **`app.jobs` keeps no `actor`, `request_id` or `correlation_id`** (`[199]` (4); MEDIUM for the design,
   nothing live). `050` stores `CTR-JOB-001`'s `tenant_context` as `workspace_id` alone
   (`050_async_kernel.sql:426-427`, `:490-510`), while RFC-2026-026 §3.5 and Q-026-8's answer source the
   worker's actor and both ids from "the job's `tenant_context`". RFC-2026-028 §3.4 proposes the columns
   (Q-028-5). Owner A0 (kernel range) with A6 and CTR-JOB-001's owner; owed before RFC-2026-026's worker half.
3. **Two records disagree on who runs the retention sweep** (`[199]` (5)). RFC-2026-017 §3 gives "retention
   sweeps" to `app_maintenance`; `service-policy-map.json` classes the retention-sweep row DISCOVERED with no

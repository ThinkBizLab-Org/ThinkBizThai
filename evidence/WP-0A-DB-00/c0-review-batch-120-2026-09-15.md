### M4 — `122`'s header makes a claim about `publish_intents` that `120`'s own grants falsify, and its probe is written so as not to test it

*Measured: read `122:32-35`, `122:136-149`, and `120:608-611`.*

`122:32-33`:

> Today this changes nothing observable on publish_intents: **no role but authenticated holds a
> privilege there**, asserted below.

`120:608-611` grants `app_worker` a thirteen-column `select` on `app.publish_intents`. The migration
header relies on that grant: `120:42-44` says *"app_worker holds SELECT here and no policy … the
worker only reads it to fan out"*. So a role other than `authenticated` does hold a privilege there,
and 122's sentence is false as written.

"Asserted below" is also not quite true. `122`'s probe 3 tests the service roles for

must, or it would fail on 120's own grant. On the pin, by contrast, the same probe explicitly

> leave the S-cell tables open so that the CARRIED policy RFC-2026-022 §7 will one day put beside
> their narrowings is not pre-empted by a closure that would have to be amended first.

The closure on `publish_intents` does exactly what that sentence forbids, one table over. The
worker's `SELECT` on the intent is the **first step of the fan-out**, and the fan-out is the `S` cell
on `app.publish_targets` that 122 deliberately leaves open. Before 122, that `SELECT` was refused by
an empty policy set — a refusal that lifts when RFC-2026-022 §7 holds. After 122 it is refused by a
`current_user = 'authenticated'` closure, which is permanent until amended. By the file's own
parallel — it says of the pin that `service-cannot-attach-a-publish-pin` is now *"a permanent
refusal, not a pending one"* — the same change happened to the intent's read and is not recorded.

I am not asking for the closure to be removed: question 11 is the Owner's and the batch implements
it faithfully. **The finding is that 122's reasoning does not match 122's effect**, and a later batch
landing the CARRIED policies will discover that it must amend `publish_intents_service_path_closed`
first — which is the one thing 122 says it arranged not to happen.

**Remedy.** Correct `122:32-33` to state what is true (`app_worker` holds column-scoped `SELECT` from
120; the closure now refuses it permanently rather than pending §7), say why the probe excepts it,
and record in `open_blockers` that the batch which lands the publisher's CARRIED policies must amend

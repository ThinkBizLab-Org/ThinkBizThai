# A0 plan: batch 105, the updated_by UPDATE forgery on seven tables (blocker 189)

Author run: `/claude/a0_atlas`. Package `WP-0A-DB-00`. Written 2026-09-27 at `main` = `5922684`. It
records what was measured and put to the Owner in session before the migration was written. The
Owner's answers are in [the disposition](product-owner-disposition-2026-09-27-batch-105.md).

## 1. Measured (private cluster at 127.0.0.1:5499, shim first, fresh `make db-migrate-clean`; `:5432` untouched)

- **17** app tables grant `authenticated` UPDATE on `updated_by`.
  - **10** of them bind it in their permissive UPDATE policy (`updated_by = (select auth.uid()) and …`).
  - **7** bind it nowhere: `business_profiles`, `industry_assignments`, `knowledge_items`,
    `page_context_profiles`, `workspace_invitations`, `workspace_settings`, `workspaces`. This
    matches A1's measurement exactly.
- A restrictive UPDATE policy with only WITH CHECK deparses with `polqual` null and WITH CHECK
  `(updated_by = ( SELECT auth.uid() AS uid))`.
- **Trial of remedy (a):** the seven policies were appended to `140_audit.sql`.
  - `rls-smoke` failed on exactly four existing positives: editors updating `business_profiles`,
    `page_context_profiles` and `knowledge_items` without naming themselves as `updated_by`.
  - The post-migrate pass failed 030's and 040's replacements by name, because their pinned
    restrictive sets had grown.
- Batches 102–104 were added on 2026-09-15, after 110 existed (2026-09-08). That is the precedent for
  a forward fix numbered inside the range it fixes.

## 2. Put to the Owner, and answered

| Question | Options | A0 recommended | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|
| Remedy | (a) restrictive UPDATE policies binding `updated_by` to the caller; (b) remove `updated_by` from the client UPDATE grant and have the database maintain it | **(a)**: the same rule the other ten tables already use, the smallest change, no new function, and it makes 102's "checked on UPDATE everywhere" true. (b) is better long-term, but only if done across all seventeen tables | `ก` |
| Grade | MEDIUM or LOW | **MEDIUM**: exploitable today, attribution an audit reads, no tenant boundary crossed | see the disposition |

## 3. The batch

1. **`105_updated_by_on_update_is_caller.sql`:** seven restrictive UPDATE policies, TO
   authenticated, no USING, WITH CHECK `updated_by = (select auth.uid())`. Its apply-time block
   asserts:
   - the seven, by exact text;
   - **a schema-wide general rule**: no table may let authenticated UPDATE `updated_by` unless an
     UPDATE policy binds it to the caller.
2. **The register:** 030's and 040's replacements pin the new names, carry `SUPERSEDED BY 105` on
   each changed line, and have their `fails_with` counts moved.
3. **The closure-text probe:** pins the seven UPDATE closures by exact text on their tables.
4. **Cases:** the four positives name their caller, and seven forging cases (one per table: the
   owner writes another member as `updated_by`) must be refused by policy. Without 105, exactly
   those seven fail (measured).
5. **Static:** the forward-fix pin test, the declared tail, the catalog snapshot tail, and floors.
6. **Blocker 189:** closed on merge.
7. **Role runs:** C0, Q0, A1.

## 5. What remains, and for whom

1. **A client-encoding change through a name computed at run time stays outside the lexer's list
   (reviewer).** `set_config('client_' || 'encoding', ...)`, or `EXECUTE` of a SET whose words are
   computed in a DO body (a SET NAMES spelled out anywhere, a literal included, is refused since the
   review round, C0 F6), changes psql's encoding with no name spelled out. In a multibyte client encoding the two
   readings part after every non-ASCII character: measured on the fed sources, 4,611 such places in
   77 of 86 files, mostly `§` before a digit in comments of integrated migrations. A byte rule would
   refuse almost the whole set; pinning PGCLIENTENCODING does not stop a mid-file change. The limit is
   written beside the rule in `psql-driver.mjs` and in the README. Owed.
2. **Schema `public` (A1 F6 on 123, Q0 F6): closed for client privileges by the review round.** The
   client privilege probe refuses any client privilege on a table in `public` or `private` and on any
   view, materialized view or foreign table in `app`, `private` or `public` (§7). What stays: a client
   may still EXECUTE an invoker function created in `public` (it bypasses nothing; a SECURITY DEFINER one
   is the definer probe's, in every schema).
3. **Remedy (b)** (105's note): drop created_by from the client INSERT grant and let the database set
   it. Not done, by the Owner's answer of 2026-10-03; it would change the grant contract on 19 tables.
4. **The permissive list is a maintenance cost by design.** Every batch that adds, drops or rewrites a
   permissive policy on a client-writable table updates `PERMISSIVE_POLICIES` in the same diff. Its
   digest in foundation-contract moves with any change, which puts the change in front of a reviewer.
5. **Restrictive policies are not in this probe.** A restrictive policy can only narrow. At `4fef70a`
   the claim that "the ones that carry a guarantee are pinned by their own probes" was true of the
   closures and of 091's and 125's pinned policies only; the other member-scope narrowings were read for
   tokens, and `... or true` passed migrate-clean (C0 F3). Since the review round all 33 narrowings are
   in `PINNED_POLICIES` by exact deparse, and the helpers they call are pinned by body (§7). 122's
   `*_service_path_closed` policies were not part of that finding and are not pinned here.
6. **The three pre-127 forging cases are kept** (the invitation case and 091's two); they now overlap
   with the new family on their tables.
7. **The RFC-2026-025 §5 points remain open** (the disposition).

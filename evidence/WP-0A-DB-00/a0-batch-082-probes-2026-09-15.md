## 1. What the batch is

`db/foundation/migrations/082_content_service_path_closed.sql`: one RESTRICTIVE policy per content
table, `for all`, **no `TO` clause** (= `PUBLIC`), `using (current_user = 'authenticated')` and the
same `with check`. Plus an apply-time block asserting (1) the five closures with exactly that shape,
read from `pg_policy` — `polpermissive` false, `polcmd = '*'`, `polroles = '{0}'`, both halves
present, identical, and reading `current_user`; (2) **the general rule S8 violates** — on every
content table, every role a permissive policy admits is bound by at least one restrictive policy;
(3) that no service role and no `anon` holds any privilege on the five tables, which is the premise
under which the batch changes nothing today; (4) that none of the three refused repairs was taken —
FORCE still on, `app_command` not an owner, `app_command` still `NOBYPASSRLS`.

It grants nothing, drops nothing, alters nothing 080 wrote. The header records the fourth repair —
naming `app_command` in a narrowing that uses 080's predicate — and why it is vacuous
(`021_member_scope.sql:389-400`: the helper is `security invoker` over a table `app_command` sees no
rows of, so it reports "not narrowed" and admits everything).

## 5. What CI must prove before any of §1 is evidence

On PostgreSQL 17 with the shim, in this PR's own run:

1. `db-migrate-clean` applies 082 after 080 and the apply-time block passes — in particular
   assertion (2), which is the first time the general rule is asked of a live catalog.
2. The five `batch-082-*` cases pass (the closure exists) **and** the suite total is 629 + 5.
3. The seven `service-cannot-*` content cases still report `deniedBy: grant` — the closure is inert
   for them because the grant refuses first, which is §1's premise (3).
4. Every `authenticated` case on the five tables is unchanged: the predicate is true for
   `authenticated`, so 080's narrowings decide exactly as before. **This is the one place the batch
   could change behaviour today**, if `as_user` set a role other than `authenticated`; measured in
   `db/foundation/test-helpers/auth-context.sql` — it sets `authenticated` — but a reading of a helper
   is not a run.
5. The five 080 negative-control entries still fire (the closure adds nothing an RLS-off control
   would notice, and must not mask what it did notice).

If any of 1–5 fails, the batch is wrong and the failure is the finding; nothing here is repaired by
having been written down.

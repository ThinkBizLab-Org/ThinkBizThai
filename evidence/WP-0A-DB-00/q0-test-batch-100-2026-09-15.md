scope_fk` and the logical key (`100_asset.sql:843-870`); §4.7's same-Workspace and same-Business

item's. For
`user_page_editor_a` the asset half of the narrowing is TRUE (case [31] proves this identity
reaches `asset_a1`'s child) and the content half is FALSE (080's
`pinned-editor-a-cannot-see-the-content-version-of-a-sibling-target-item` proves it). A case on

`asset_a1_sibling_page`/`asset_version_a1_sibling_page` linked to `content_version_a1` — is
